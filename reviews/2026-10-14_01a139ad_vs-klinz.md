## Lessons
1. **Ink-short hands change the mulligan.** With uninkables in hand, the 6-drop refill is the card to send back,
   not the cheap curve (H-1 caveat).
2. **Opposing characters are free singers** (RULE-9). Tod, Sven and Milo between them sang three songs for free in
   this game. Count their 3+ / 5+ cost characters when they have Malicious, Mean, and Scary or Above the Crowd.
3. **Against Emerald/Amethyst, expect the Malicious, Mean, and Scary + Prince Phillip wipe from 9 ink.** Don't
   deploy characters the turn before that unless they'll get value first. Rebuild right after (turn 25 did this
   well).
4. **Before digging because nothing looks good, run the race, and count their sweepers** (H-4).
5. **When choosing what to sacrifice, compare what's left on board and in hand afterwards** (TRADE-1). A damaged
   1-drop is cheaper than a growing Scrooge.
6. **Dumbo timing:** play him with spare ink and a dry Evasive ally, so he pays off on arrival. Don't overpay for
   the setup. Against "3 {S} or less" removal, expect him to go quickly (`kb/cards/`).
7. **Ward blanks "chosen" exerts** (Isis, Shadow). Effects that don't choose, like Demona – Scourge's exert-all, still work.
8. **Extra cards matter less to an opponent with a full hand and limited ink.** That's when "let them draw 2" over
   losing a body is most acceptable (H-2 converse).

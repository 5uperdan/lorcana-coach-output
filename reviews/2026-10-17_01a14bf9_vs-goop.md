## Lessons
1. **Time "draw until N" refills for when your hand is empty** (H-1, refined four times in this game).
   - On the play with one card a turn, a 7-card hand runs out about turn 6.
   - The cap absorbs earlier draws, so drawing early buys timing and consistency, not extra cards.
   - Before a refill, spare cards are effectively free ink. But that doesn't apply to engines or must-answer threats (Dumbo).
   - Check the opponent's hand size before casting, because the refill feeds them too.
2. **Once later turns will be short of cards rather than ink, unspent ink isn't waste.** Don't spend a card just to use ink (H-2).
3. **Against Amber/Sapphire's Madrigal package, don't hand them damage they can heal.**
   - Chip damage and counter-damage become cards (Ohana, FIND THAT VISION) or lore (Isabela – Caring Cultivator).
   - Don't quest with a character whose counter-damage would become their lore.
   - Kill Isabela – Caring Cultivator first.
4. **Their main removal only hits characters with 5+ {S}.** Plan around which of your characters are exposed: here, Demona – Scourge, and Wasabi when you hold an ink drop.
5. **Respect Raging Storm once an Amber opponent reaches 8 ink, but don't freeze.** Odds table in `kb/meta/inks.md`. Expect the wipe after their quests, not before.
6. **"Anything they play this turn can quest next turn."** Count every character they have, and every location with lore, when working out what must die.
7. **Quest first, then remove (or wipe),** and pay ink for songs rather than singing when your characters are worth more questing. Both players did this to good effect.
8. **Use repeatable effects as a reaction, not pre-emptively** (H-3, open). The user thinks it's too general to promote yet.

## Hindsight
Doesn't change the verdicts above.
- **The swings were theirs:** two Raging Storms (turns 14, 18) wiped 7 of your characters, and the heal-for-lore line gave them +4 on turn 22.
- **The game was nearly lost twice:**
  - **Turn 27:** all four of their questers had to die.
  - **Turn 29:** Casa had to go before their Set step.

  Both were solved.
- **Without hindsight, this was a defensible decision that the dice punished:** Bruno's self-heal on turn 14 turned your turn-13 chip damage into a FIND THAT VISION.
- **Ohana Means Family** turned the 2 damage you moved onto Bruno on turn 11 into 2 cards. You couldn't have known they had it. It's now recorded as matchup knowledge.
- **What won:** card flow (Guidebook ×2, Hades, four Weights draws) against an opponent who ran out of cards, Wasabi's TWIN BLADES, and precise removal turns.
